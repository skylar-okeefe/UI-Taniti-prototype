GETTING TO THE ISLAND

    BY AIR
  • Current Service: Taniti is served by a local regional airport accommodating propeller planes and small jets.
  • Future Upgrades: An airport expansion project is currently underway. Within the next few years, the facility will accommodate larger commercial jets.
    BY SEA
  • Cruise Docking: A small cruise ship docks weekly at Yellow Leaf Bay for a one-night stay.
